About

Monisha Aroumougam is an independent artist who creates pencil sketches, colour pencil pieces, resin art and handmade crafts. She enjoys working with texture, subtle colour palettes and sometimes experiments with watercolour & acrylic for personal projects.

Open for commissions in all mediums including Watercolour and Acrylics.

Location: Pondicherry, India · Languages: English, Tamil
